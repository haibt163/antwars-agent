## Core idea: build, then strike

The economy exists to fund an army that ends the match. Do not keep adding workers once income and population are strong, and do not sit in defence waiting for the territory clock. Three win paths, one army: (a) strip enemy-held ground and reach the queens, (b) hold the 6× territory ratio for 300 s, (c) lead on kills when the live match limit arrives (`time_up`, decided on kills; read `ticksLeft`/`secondsLeft` — 18,000/27,000/36,000 ticks, or 1,800/2,700/3,600 s, set by the map config, so do not assume 1,800 s; VERIFIED, live rules). Pick (c) deliberately; see the kill race below.

## Opening and the queen clock

- **Start clock: zero.** `scripts/open-match.mjs` seats the match server during the nest window and makes the single `pick_nest` attempt, `set_brood workers` and the first FORAGE mark itself, before the first tick; the model must not repeat or race those commands. You inherit a colony that was hatched and foraging from tick 0, with its note in `%USERPROFILE%\.antwars\opener.log`. Verify the `nest_pick`, `brood` and `forage` lines there and fix only a refusal; everything below assumes that opening landed.
- Extra queens (rules identical in all versions seen, CONFIRMED): earliest at tick 3,000 (5:00); cost 400 food, doubling per live extra (400, 800, 1,600); 180 s cooldown; the tile must be held by us and at least 32 tiles from every standing nest, ours or the enemy's (32 view tiles = 16 reference tiles × grid scale 2; VERIFIED, live rules 2026-11-09).
- Refusals seen most: `queen_tile_not_held` (308 of 520), then `queen_too_close` (133). Prepare before 5:00: from about 4:00 choose a tile in our own half at least 34 tiles from our home queen (margin; the exact metric is not stated), keep ants on it (held tiles decay after 20 s unvisited), and keep 400 food unspent. Try at tick 3,000–3,300.
- If refused, read the code and fix the cause once: not held means put ants on that tile and retry; too close means move farther away. Do not repeat an unchanged request.
- Targets: first extra at 5:00–5:30; second at 8:00–9:00 (cooldown makes 8:00 the earliest; archive median 9:07); a third (1,600 food) only on the signal trigger below. Being a single-queen colony past about 6:00 was the common thread in our losses; the Twin Shields win ended with one extra queen ever founded and 2,341 food banked.
- Third extra signal trigger (UNVERIFIED strategy; no fixed time): found it only when all three hold — 1,600 food banked, a held tile at least 32 tiles from every nest, and the enemy field army is out of sight (or our army holds the lane). ## Early rush guard (experimental)

The Three Crossings v2 loss shows one 100-soldier mark at 3:50 can end a one-queen game by 7:24. In the archive a first ATTACK before 3:00 happened in 53 matches.

- Start mixed or soldier brood by about 3:30 so a guard of roughly 15–25 soldiers (UNVERIFIED sizing) is near the home queen by 4:00, held with a DEFEND mark that is refreshed before its 180 s lifetime ends. Return to worker-first brood after the second queen is up.
  - 2026-11-10 Central Lake v2 win (house `antwars/gpt-6-astra`): the 3:30 mixed switch left 8 guard soldiers at tick 2,482 while the enemy fielded 77 soldiers by tick 3,005, so a 4:00 guard below about 15 is thin against a house bot's 5:00 wave (counts VERIFIED, `study/match-win-2026-11-10-central-lake-v2.md`; whether 8 was *insufficient* is UNVERIFIED — that wave met our forward ants and no queen of ours was touched).
- If any enemy ant is seen near the nest earlier, switch to mixed or soldier brood at once.
- Treat any queen HP loss as urgent. A second queen turns a lost raid into a lost 40 HP, not a lost game.
- Guard every queen, not only the home nest. The Twin Shields extra queen had a 4-worker DEFEND from tick 1,996; the first enemy contact at tick 8,079 killed it by tick 8,162 (CONFIRMED). Once enemy ants are seen within about 20 tiles of any queen, put a soldier DEFEND mark (about 15 or more) on that queen and refresh it before its 180 s lifetime ends.

## Command budget

The live limit is one command per 10 s, and marks last 180 s (CONFIRMED in `rules.config`; 624 `rate_limited` refusals in the archive). That is about 18 commands per mark lifetime, shared by `order`, `count_mark` and `found_queen`; `set_brood` and `observe` do not use this allowance (they count only toward the 2-calls/s limit). Spend the slots in this order: queen safety, founding, refreshing the marks that matter, then everything else. Do not retry a refused command unchanged.

`SPAWN` and `REMOVE` are verbs of the `count_mark` tool (`REMOVE` + `markId` from `view.myMarks`; `SPAWN` + `queenId`, `tileX`, `tileY`), not separate tools.

## Soldier surge (economy-to-army conversion)

Set the mix before the cap. At the cap new ants only replace the dead, so brood changes then produce nothing until ants die. In Open Field our colony stood at 449–512 ants by tick 14,395 (the cap is reachable from tick 12,606; it was NOT at the cap at tick 11,857, CONFIRMED from the log). Workers rarely die and soldiers die in every strike, so a colony that is worker-heavy at the cap stays worker-heavy. Hatch cadence is CONFIRMED: one worker per 10 ticks per worker queen, one soldier per 20 ticks per soldier queen, so one worker queen out-produces a soldier queen two to one, and two soldier queens plus one worker queen hatch about half workers. No ant carries a caste in the public log, so every worker and soldier count in our reports is an estimate; the agent's census read 386 workers and 125 soldiers at tick 12,600 (a colony-wide total of 511 that matches the log, but an unverifiable split).

Schedule (reference numbers, UNVERIFIED):

- Queen 0 on workers (mixed during the early rush guard). Queen 1 on soldiers from founding.
- Queen 2 on workers only while food is short. Switch it to soldiers once stored food is about 1,000 or more and rising at two successive observations, and in any case by about 12:00 (tick 7,200 on an 18,000-tick match). In Open Field it stayed on workers until tick 16,137, long after the colony reached 449–512 ants, with 5,543 food banked at tick 14,049 (the agent's status; the public log has no food field).
- After that every queen except queen 0 is on soldiers; keep queen 0 on workers unless the worker floor is breached. In the 11/06 Four Lanes win queen 0 stayed on workers from tick 3,112 to the end while queens 1–3 bred soldiers, and the colony finished at 363 soldiers / 405 workers of 768 (47% soldiers in sight; VERIFIED, `study/match-win-2026-11-06-four-lanes.md`). Keep the other queens on soldiers through the cap so each loss is replaced by a soldier.
- Worker floor: put one queen back on workers only if workers fall below about a third of the cap or income clearly collapses (Wide Plain ended with 0 workers).
- Soldier target at the cap: about 40% of the live cap (512/768/1024 by config). Benchmarks: 11/04 Four Lanes 299 of 768 = 39% at the last count, and 11/06 Four Lanes 363 of 768 = 47% in sight at the end. Up to 50% is EXPERIMENTAL, for a planned kill race. The Open Field soldier share is unknown (no caste in the log); the agent's estimate was about 24% at tick 12,600. Treat the share as a cap/concentration target, not a damage-efficiency gain: per 6 food, 2 workers have the same 8 HP and more damage/s (2.0 vs 1.5; 2.5 vs 1.875 on our ground) and can carry, while a soldier gives 2× HP per cap slot and one death instead of two for the same HP (VERIFIED, live rules 2026-11-09).
- Banked food has one real sink: more queens. The price doubles with each live extra (400, 800, 1,600, 3,200). Re-price the next queen at every observation after the second queen is up. In Three Crossings v2 the fourth extra (3,200) was affordable from tick 10,165 (3,472 food and rising) and was never attempted. A queen at or near the cap adds replacement speed and one more life (INFERRED), so found it whenever it is affordable and a legal held tile exists; below the cap, turn banked food into queens first, then soldiers.
- Record worker and soldier counts at every observation and call them in-sight estimates unless every ant is visible. The 3:1 and 5:1 figures quoted earlier are not confirmed by any log.
- Unmarked soldiers do not advance (VERIFIED, live rules 2026-11-09): a free soldier holds within 6 tiles of where it became free — its hatch tile, or the spawn tile it walked to — fights only enemies in its own 12-tile sight, and pushes only when local friends are at least 0.6× the enemies (otherwise it falls back to the nearest queen); it never pursues a queen. Treat unmarked soldiers as home garrison, and move soldiers forward only with `SPAWN` before they hatch or with a mark.

## Concentrated push

- Stage at the edge of our own half in the chosen lane's y-range (our win used (100,22) on Central Lake v2), by setting the soldier queen's spawn point there. Then step ATTACK marks along ONE lane toward the enemy. The Four Lanes win stepped west along y≈80: (55,80) 26, (42,80) 30, (26,80) 40 soldiers.
- Mass first (67 soldiers within one 10×10 block at tick 7,873 on Central Lake v2). Prefer forward enemy groups and foragers (Four Lanes: a lane group of 23 workers and 3 soldiers fell for 4 losses). Avoid attacking into dense defenders on held ground (INFERRED from the 10-match archive). Carrying workers never bite ants or queens and walk past enemies to deliver: enemy foragers are free damage, and our own foragers are never a screen (VERIFIED, live rules 2026-11-09).
- Deploy the army you have, and keep it fed. Open Field's mid-match marks show the pool running dry: the reinforcement mark on the queen at tick 7,251 requested 60 soldiers and claimed 0, and the one at tick 9,350 requested 70 and claimed 40 (CONFIRMED; the cause is INFERRED: the earlier marks at (52,24) and (52,29) already held 120). Late in the match the marks asked for only 24–40 soldiers while the colony held 449–512 ants (CONFIRMED). So do both: run the surge earlier, and once it has run put about 70% of live soldiers on one lane with the rest as home reserve and queen guards.
- Marks bleed. A mark keeps claiming replacements for its whole 1,800-tick life. In Open Field only 4 of 18 ATTACK marks finished with more enemy deaths than our losses within 6 tiles of the mark (INFERRED); the mark at (51,55) took 133 of our ants for 73 enemy deaths over 1,799 ticks. Check the trade after each mark and do not re-lay a losing one at the same size. The per-mark credits quoted in the Open Field report could not be reproduced from the log (UNVERIFIED).
- A mark's soldiers are not free until it is REMOVEd or expires (1,800 ticks). REMOVE costs a command slot (one per 10 s) and is refused (`bad_mark_removal`) once the mark has expired, so redirecting the army takes at least two slots. On 11/06 marks laid at ticks 8,204, 12,223 and 12,834 claimed 5, 0 and 0 soldiers because older marks held about 175 (run log). Keep at most three ATTACK marks live, read the free-soldier count before laying one, remove the stale mark first, and lay the queen mark directly on the located queen.
- Keep a home reserve; do not strip home ground for kills. Territory decays when ants leave: in the Twin Shields win two qualifying territory runs were cancelled (at ticks 9,802 and 10,101) after the army pushed west, and the run that counted began at 10,451. Before the army leaves, lay stationary ground-holding marks across home tiles; the report says the run restarted once the colony held more than 100 tiles (threshold UNVERIFIED; check live rules).
- Put the ATTACK mark ON the queen tile. Both enemy queens that died fell within 357 and 179 ticks of a mark laid within 1 tile of them (CONFIRMED, Twin Shields). Take a fresh `observe` immediately before each mark. A mark tile must be within sight of a living ant, 12 tiles (CONFIRMED in the 11/06 public log: `sightTiles` 12, `countMarks.placement` `colony_sight`, refusal text "within sight of a living ant"; `markReachTiles` 20 and `smellTiles` 16 are other settings and do not govern placement; marks display within 16 tiles). A tile 12.17 tiles away needed an intermediate step on 11/06. `count_mark_not_visible` was 47% of all 2,316 refusals.
- Scouting (VERIFIED mechanics): send the up-to-six-ant squad with one `order go_to` per leg (one command; reveals 12 tiles around it). A 3–4 ant count mark is heavier: REMOVE plus a lay per step, holds those ants for 180 s, and as ATTACK uses one of the three live-ATTACK slots. Prefer squad legs, one per observation, and give a seen queen the next slot. (Live rules: `order` steers the squad, so a lone free ant is not controllable, but the squad is. The mark variant is UNVERIFIED and not recommended.)

## Queen hunt

- The enemy start queen is somewhere in its own start box, weighted toward the box centre (for example (16.5,48.5) on the 96-tile maps), but on 11/06 it stood at (29,19), near the box's north-east corner (CONFIRMED, public log), and it is NOT at the mirror of ours (Central Lake v2: ours (112.5,28.5), theirs (20.5,21.5)). Enemy extras can sit at the map edge: the 11/06 public log shows extras at (4,46), founded at tick 4,990, and at (1.5,2.5) in the far north-west corner, founded at tick 6,927, which we never saw. Extras usually follow the map playbook but not always. Scout inside the enemy box with the squad (or stepped marks), then re-observe before ordering.
- Count the enemy's queens. In the Twin Shields win the enemy had three (start (18.5,23.5), (36.5,68.5) founded at tick 3,029, forward (56.5,22.5) founded at tick 4,877). We killed two, and the one in the playbook's south cell survived untouched. After one lane is cleared, sweep the playbook's other cell with stepped marks before settling into a hold.
- **Hard trigger (VERIFIED mechanics):** when a queen is seen, the next command slot goes to a mark on her tile if a living ant is within 12 tiles; otherwise to the nearest legal tile toward her (within 12 tiles of an ant). No founding, sweep step or REMOVE first. Record tile, HP and tick; put at least 10 soldiers next to her. Stripping enemy-held tiles also blocks their founding. An ant damages a queen only when no enemy ant is within 2 tiles of it and the ant is not carrying, so clear her guards inside 2 tiles first: a strike that reaches her with ants still adjacent does zero queen damage (VERIFIED, live rules 2026-11-09).
- A damaged queen regenerates 1 HP per 10 s only when no enemy ant is within 6 tiles at the check (live rule). VERIFIED in the Open Field log: the queen at (52.5,30.5) was struck from about 39.5 HP to 2.65 HP in about 15 s (ticks 7,162–7,307), then sat at 2.65 HP for over 1,100 ticks, regained only 7 HP in 259 s because our ants were near her about half the time, and died at tick 10,323 when 15 of our ants reached her (9.5 HP to 0 in 39 ticks). The strike mass is not the problem; staying on her is. Lay the next mark on her tile while the first is still alive, keep about 20 soldiers free (unclaimed by other marks) to claim it, and keep ants within 6 tiles until the server shows her dead.
- The server does not show us enemy queen deaths (no enemy `queen_lost` in eight history pages on 11/06). A queen that disappears from `enemyQueens` while our ants hold her tile is presumed dead (INFERRED; on 11/06 the public log confirmed the (29,19) queen died at tick 8,488); one that leaves sight because we moved away is not. Do not page history to confirm: on 11/06 about 800 ticks passed between the tick-8,741 observation and the next command while eight history pages and a rate-limited call went on that.
- When no enemy queen is in sight, search; do not hold. On 11/06 ticks 8,741–12,777 (6.7 minutes) passed with no mark aimed at a located queen: the army mopped up, swept toward the box centre (about (16,48); an extra queen stood at (4,46) and another in the far north-west corner at (1.5,2.5)) and held ground with 180 workers, while the playbook's top cell (32,72) was first swept at tick 14,246 (a mark at (30,70)) and held the last queen. Sweep the playbook cells in order of n (Three Crossings v2: (32,72), then (48,16)), then the box corners and edges, then the box centre (on 11/06 two of the four enemy queens stood at the west edge or a corner; one match), one mark per cell, each within 12 tiles of the army. Place ground-holding marks only after the next search mark is down.
- Hold and hunt are not exclusive. Once nothing of the enemy is in sight and its territory is 0, keep the 300 s hold running with ground-holding marks and send the rest of the army to sweep for the remaining queens, keeping a soldier DEFEND on each of our queens. Abandon the sweep only if it empties home ground. A reachable queen kill ends the match sooner than the hold (queens_killed); the Twin Shields hold took 3,000 ticks after the enemy had no ants.

## Kill race (experimental)

- If neither side wins by queens or by a 300 s territory hold, `time_up` goes to the side with more kills (Open Field, `decidedBy: kills`, margin 34 of 4,244 deaths). The public log gives exact per-colony kills after the match (deaths by colony) but no per-mark credits; whether the live `observe` or status shows kill totals is UNVERIFIED, so check at the start of the run. If it does not, rely on the time trigger below and judge trades from losses and enemy ants seen.
- Trigger: the 6× territory ratio is not established and not safely progressing, and the live `ticksLeft` is about 330 s or less (the extra 30 s over the 300 s hold is an EXPERIMENTAL margin, not a game rule), or earlier when a visible kill count shows us clearly behind. In Open Field the 6× ratio was met in ten runs but never held for 300 s (CONFIRMED). Do not abandon a territory hold that is safely progressing.
- In kill race: keep a soldier DEFEND on every queen and enough home ground to deny a territory win; put most soldiers on ONE lane; prefer good trades (enemy workers and foragers, isolated groups, a queen we can finish); send no small waves into a defended position; stop marks whose losses exceed their kills; re-observe before each mark; keep going until the server confirms the result.
- Last resort (UNVERIFIED trade quality): if soldiers are too few and food is banked, an ATTACK with caste `any` can draft workers.

## 1v1 map playbook (we are colony 1 on the right; if seated as colony 0, mirror x: 127−x, or 255−x on Wide Plain)

Source: `study/maps.md`. Water is fixed per map; rocks are random each match and never sealed a lane. Boxes: standard maps enemy x0–31 and ours x96–127; Wide Plain enemy x0–63 and ours x192–255. Enemy extra-queen cells are the 8×8 cell whose top-left is (x,y), from founding events by the left colony (CONFIRMED, n shown). The lane to push is INFERRED: the lane that leads to the commonest enemy extra cell. The cells are house-model habits, so treat them as search priors and update from sightings; a harness agent may place queens elsewhere.

- **Overall:** the enemy's first extra queen is in the southern half of its side in 149 of 253 cases (my recount from `study/maps.md` Appendix C); on Central Lake, Isthmus, Three Crossings and Twin Shields it is south in about 2 of 3 or more. ## Decision rule

At each observation, prioritize:

1. Prevent an immediate queen loss.
2. Stop an imminent enemy territory win or defend critical home ground.
3. Found the next extra queen when gate, cooldown, food and a held tile allow.
4. Restore worker income and sustainable production.
5. Correct exposed queens, broken marks or vulnerable expansion positions.
6. Run the soldier surge on schedule; if 1–5 are stable, assemble most soldiers and push on one lane, with ground-holding marks left at home; prioritise exposed queens and sweep the playbook cells for every remaining one.
7. Switch to kill race when the territory route is not safely progressing and the live `ticksLeft` is about 330 s or less.
8. Otherwise expand, scout and take selective trades.

At every observation, including during a hunt, also check: brood on every queen, the next queen's price against stored food, free soldiers and stale marks. In the 11/06 Three Crossings v2 win all four lapsed once the hunt began.

This is a priority order, not a rigid build order. Do not trade a reliable territory win for a risky queen chase.

**No strategy guarantees a win.**
